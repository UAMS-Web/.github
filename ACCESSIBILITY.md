# Accessibility

UAMS is committed to making its digital content fully accessible. As a public institution, UAMS is required to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA by April 26, 2027, under the U.S. Department of Justice Title II ADA final rule (April 2024).

For the full commitment and the resources that support it, see:

- [Accessibility resources](https://communications.uams.edu/web/kb/accessibility-resources/)
- [Web Accessibility](https://communications.uams.edu/web/web-accessibility/)

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

- **A problem on a UAMS website:** [contact Web Services](https://communications.uams.edu/web/contact-web-services/). Tell them the page address, what you were trying to do, and what got in the way.
- **A problem in a repository's code:** open an issue in that repository and apply the `accessibility` label. Describe the barrier, the assistive technology or browser you used, and the steps to reproduce it.
